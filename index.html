    How a project runs

    Four steps from first message to launch. You see progress at every stage.

    1. 01

      Discover

      A short call about your goals, audience and deadline. We agree on scope and price before any work starts.

    2. 02

      Design

      Moodboards, wireframes or a storyboard first, so you can steer the direction early, while changes are easy.

    3. 03

      Build & edit

      We build the site or cut the video and share previews as we go, with review rounds built in.

    4. 04

      Launch

      Your site goes live or your files arrive in every format you need, with a walkthrough of what you got.
